4. Criteria

Enrollment
Inclusion
1. Healthy adults aged 19 to 50 years at the time of screening (for Part B, males only).
2. Individuals with a body weight between 50.0 kg and 90.0 kg at the time of screening, and a body mass index (BMI) between 18.0 and 27.0.
 BMI (kg/m2) = Weight (kg) / {Height (m)} 2
3. Individuals without any skin conditions or damage (including scars or tattoos) at the site of administration.
4. Individuals deemed suitable for this trial by the investigator based on physical examination, vital signs, 12-lead ECG, and clinical laboratory tests.
5. Individuals who have listened to a detailed explanation of the trial, fully understood it, voluntarily decided to participate, and provided written consent prior to the screening examination.
Exclusion
1. Individuals with clinically significant diseases affecting the liver, kidneys, nervous system, immune system, respiratory system, endocrine system, blood, or tumors; or a history of cardiovascular diseases, mental disorders (such as mood disorders, obsessive-compulsive disorder, etc.), who are over the age of 19 at the time of screening (for Part B, males only).
2. Individuals with a history of clinically significant hypersensitivity reactions.
3. Individuals with the following results during screening tests:
· Positive results in serum tests (for hepatitis B, hepatitis C, human immunodeficiency virus (HIV), syphilis).
· For Part B: Testosterone levels outside the normal range (2.67 – 10.12 ng/mL).
· For Part B: Leutinizing hormone (LH) levels outside the normal range (1.0 – 5.3 mIU/mL).
· For Part B: Follicular stimulating hormone (FSH) levels outside the normal range (1.3 – 8.1 mIU/mL).
4. Individuals with a history of drug abuse or who tested positive for abuse substances in urine drug screening tests.
5. Individuals who have taken any prescription or herbal medicines within 2 weeks before the first scheduled dose, or any over-the-counter medications (OTCs), dietary supplements, or vitamin supplements within 1 week before the first scheduled dose (unless otherwise deemed appropriate by the investigator).
